Welcome to the Li Laboratory

The Li Laboratory investigates organoid intelligence for translational medicine. We combine induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC)-derived organoids with flexible nanoelectronics, multimodal electrophysiological recording and stimulation, spatial multi-omics, and AI-assisted analysis to understand disease development and progression and guide new therapeutic strategies.
